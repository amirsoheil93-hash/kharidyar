KharidYar – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

KharidYar is a shared household shopping-list app. This page explains what data the app handles. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address (or the private relay address Apple provides) and a user ID, used to sign you in.
  • Name: the display name you choose, shown to other members of your household.
  • Shopping content: household names, invite codes and the items you add (name, quantity, aisle, priority, checked state).
  • Device data: a push-notification token and your app language, used to notify household members when items are added.

How it is used

Only to run the app: sign you in, sync your list with the people in your household, and send notifications about newly added items. Data is visible only to members of the same household.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with our hosting provider Supabase (EU region, Frankfurt). Sign in with Apple and push notifications are provided by Apple. Some data (cached list, suggestions, queued offline changes) is also kept on your device.

Deleting your data

In the app open Household settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your memberships and your push tokens. A household is deleted with its items once its last member has left. You can also email us and we will delete your data.

Children

KharidYar is not directed at children under 13.
